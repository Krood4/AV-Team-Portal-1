Lyft workspaces give employees access to laptop presentation, Google Meet, and live streaming — all through a simple self-service Crestron touchscreen at the podium.

How-to guides

Running the system for your use case

Select your mode below for a step-by-step guide on getting set up.

1
Wake the system & select Laptop mode
  • At the podium, tap the Crestron tablet to wake it up from sleep.
  • Select Laptop from the top menu.
  • All TVs and projectors will turn on automatically.
2
Connect your computer
  • Plug in your computer via Thunderbolt or USB-C adapter and wait a few seconds for the screens to react.
  • By default, your computer outputs mirrored screens — your laptop shows the same content as projectors and TVs.
    • To use your laptop for presenter notes, go to System Preferences → Displays and deselect Mirror Displays.
3
Set up audio & volume
  • For video sound, go to System Preferences → Sound and ensure Crestron is selected. If using the AUX jack, choose Headphones.
  • Use the volume sliders at the bottom of the Crestron tablet:
    • Program — controls volume from HDMI and AUX Jack
    • Mics — controls microphone volume
  • The Mic Mute option mutes the mic both in the room and on any Hangouts call.
4
Wrap up
  • When finished, press the Off button on the Crestron to shut down the system.
  • Place the mics back on the charging cradle.
1
Wake the system & join your meeting
  • At the podium, tap the Crestron tablet to wake it up from sleep.
  • Once on the Google Meet screen, use the directional arrows to select your event from the calendar, or type your meeting name using the virtual keyboard.
2
Set up presentations
  • If there are presentations, the presenter must go to go.lyft.com/present and dial into the Hangouts to share their screen on the projectors, TVs, and to remote attendees.
3
Control volume
  • Use the Program volume slider at the bottom of the tablet to control remote presenter volume (left = lower, right = louder).
  • Mic volume controls both the in-room sound and the Google Meet audio.
  • Selecting Mic Mute will prevent the room from being heard on Hangouts.
4
Wrap up
  • When finished, press the Off button to shut down the system.
  • Place the mics back on the charging cradle.
Some spaces allow events to be streamed to IBM Cloud Video (Ustream), letting your audience watch in full audio and video quality.
1
Turn on the system & enable streaming
  • Turn the system on and select either Laptop or VC.
  • On the bottom right of the Crestron panel, hit the Streaming/Rec "On" button.
2
Go live
  • A pop-up will appear with the off-timer warning and the link for viewers to watch the stream.
  • Tap "Got it, Start streaming!" to go live.
! Streams have a 3-hour maximum. If your event runs longer, turn streaming off and back on during a break to restart the timer.
3
Monitor your stream
  • You are now live streaming! The input selected for the Right Projector determines how the stream looks:
    • If Video Conf is selected — the stream is a fullscreen of the Video Conference.
    • If Laptop is selected — the stream shows the deck with the camera shot superimposed over it.
4
End the stream & access your recording
  • When done, press the Off button on the Crestron panel.
  • A ticket to IT will be required to access your recording — file one at go.lyft.com/itsupport.
